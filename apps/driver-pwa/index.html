Location sharing
While this trip is in progress your phone's location is shared with the farmer, the FPO, your fleet owner and the destination mandi. Sharing stops automatically when you end the trip. Nothing is tracked outside a trip.

Before you drive

  • Mount the phone where it can see the sky and plug it into the charger.
  • Keep this app on screen. In a browser, location stops while the screen is off, during calls, or while another app (like Maps) is in front. The farmer sees "location paused" until you come back.
  • Don't press the power button. The app keeps the screen on while tracking.
  • Allow location While using the app and allow notifications. The "AgriPulse: tracking on" notification stays until you end the trip.
  • You can lock the phone or use Maps: location keeps being shared until you end the trip.
  • Set battery to Unrestricted for AgriPulse Driver (Settings → Apps → AgriPulse Driver → Battery). On Xiaomi, Oppo, Vivo and Realme also turn on Autostart.
